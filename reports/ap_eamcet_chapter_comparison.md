# AP EAMCET chapter-wise collection and comparison

Verified on 2026-10-04 against the existing year-wise question database.

- **93 unchanged Selfstudys chapter PDFs:** Physics 29, Maths 30, Chemistry 34; 4,367 original PDF pages.
- The source URLs are exclusively in the [AP EAMCET chapter-wise section](https://www.selfstudys.com/books/ap-eamcet-previous-year-paper/english/chapter-wise). No JEE, TS EAMCET, Biology, or year-wise Selfstudys downloads are in these three folders.
- **17,359 existing Maths/Physics/Chemistry archive records checked**, including Engineering and Agriculture/Pharmacy where these subjects occur. Botany and Zoology are outside the requested subjects.
- **1,251 archive records confidently matched** to chapter question stems with matching exam date/shift and numeral tokens.
- **16,108 unmatched or uncertain records retained** in 189 PDFs under `chapter-wise/Missing questions/`, grouped by subject/year/stream. These PDFs retain original question and option regions from PDFs already in this repository, with source labels and bookmarks.
- **Every relevant archive record has an entry** pointing either to a strong chapter match or to its retained original region in the supplement. The sets are disjoint and their union equals all 17,359 relevant archive IDs.

## Limits of the comparison

Selfstudys chapter PDFs have outlined text, so OCR is necessary for comparison. OCR can lose or misread formulas. Only strong, unambiguous stem matches within the same exam session with identical numeral tokens count as covered. Everything else is retained rather than discarded. **“Missing questions” therefore includes uncertain matches as well as likely omissions; it is not a certified list of questions absent from Selfstudys.**

The detected chapter question exam years are 2021, 2022, 2024, 2025. The chapter collection alone does not establish complete 2015–2026 coverage. Earlier/later sessions and subjects in the year-wise archive remain available through the supplement where unmatched.

All 85 retained records whose existing database question crops were incomplete were recovered from native question-number/ID boundaries in the original PDFs. No whole-page fallback is required for these indexed records.

## Unindexed originals and database gap

Two additional scanned 2016 AP EAMCET papers in this repository have **zero indexed questions**. Their relevant original pages are also retained as five subject PDFs: Engineering Maths (original pages 1–28), Physics (29–45), Chemistry (46–61); Agriculture/Pharmacy Physics (40–51) and Chemistry (52–62). Their printed subject headings and the 81/121 subject transitions were checked visually and with OCR. Biology pages are excluded. The original scans, including formulas, diagrams, Telugu and source marks, are preserved.

The indexed 2016 Engineering paper has 159 records and no record numbered **105 (Physics)**. The additional scanned 2016 Engineering Physics section includes the original booklet's question range 81–120. It is retained conservatively; these unindexed source variants can duplicate questions already present elsewhere and have not been certified as exact per-question matches.

The 17,359-record match/retention partition applies to the searchable database. These five original sections separately account for the papers omitted from that database. Existing source/date/shift uncertainties remain disclosed in the original archive reports; no missing dates, questions, or answers are invented.
